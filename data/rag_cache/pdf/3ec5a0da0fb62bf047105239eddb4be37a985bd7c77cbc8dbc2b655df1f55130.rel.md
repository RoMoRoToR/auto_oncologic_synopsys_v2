## Design

A randomised, open label, two treatment, three period, three sequence, single dose, partial replicate pivotal bioequivalence study was carried out under fasted conditions in 42 healthy male subjects, aged 19-42 years. As per pilot study data and literature review, the intra subject CV of ezetimibe was found more than 30%, and hence the partial replicate study design was chosen. The reference treatment was  administered  twice  to  assess  variability.  Treatment  1  was  a  single  dose  of  one  fixed  dose combination  of  rosuvastatin  10  mg  and  ezetimibe  10  mg  tablet.  Treatment  2  consisted  of  a rosuvastatin 10 mg film-coated tablet and an ezetimibe 10 mg film-coated tablet taken concomitantly.
